# ADR 20: Marketing Fund Allocation

## Changelog

## Status
Draft

## Context
Thorchain is leading decentralized liquidity protocol that facilitates cross-chain native swaps and is integrated in numerous wallets and front end platforms. Despite its technical innovations and widespread utility, there is currently no dedicated fund allocation for marketing efforts. This lack of marketing support limits overall protocol awareness, which in turn reduces the ability to attract new users, and ecosystem growth. As a result, Thorchain's full potential remains underutilized, especially among less-informed audiences who are not yet familiar with its capabilities.

## Proposal
This ADR proposes the creation of a dedicated marketing fund, sourced from a small portion of the protocol’s existing revenue streams or treasury. The fund will support initiatives aimed at promoting the Thorchain ecosystem and its integrations. Marketing campaigns will highlight Thorchain's unique features and benefits to increase adoption and engagement across the crypto industry.

### Key Benefits of Marketing
1. **Increased Adoption**:
   Marketing educates users about the protocol’s features, driving adoption among traders, swappers, liquidity providers, and developers.

2. **Community Growth**:
   Targeted campaigns can attract new users and developers to existing interfaces, fostering a stronger and more diverse community around Thorchain.

3. **Ecosystem Awareness**:
   By showcasing integrations with popular wallets and dApps, marketing can enhance awareness of Thorchain’s utility in the broader DeFi space.

4. **Brand Reputation**:
   Consistent marketing efforts can establish Thorchain as a leader in decentralized liquidity, improving its reputation and trust among stakeholders.

5. **Long-Term Sustainability**:
   Educating users about Thorchain’s innovations (e.g. native swaps) strengthens its position in a competitive DeFi landscape, leading to more sustainable growth.

### Proposed Marketing Initiatives
1. **Hire Marketing Company **:
   - Partner with at least one professional marketing company, that specialises in the crypto industry, to product and action a broad marketing strategy to grow overall awareness and understanding of Thorchain and its associated products. This marketing strategy could include:

   - Produce and distrubute blogs, videos, and infographics explaining Thorchain’s technology and benefits.
   - Launch referral programs and trading competitions to incentivize user engagement.
   - Host and distribute webinars, interviews and AMA sessions with the core team and community leaders.
   - Collaborate with wallets and dApps that integrate Thorchain for joint promotional efforts.
   - Highlight case studies showcasing successful use cases and integrations.
   - Represent Thorchain at blockchain conferences and hackathons to increase visibility.
   - Sponsor industry events to attract the attention of institutional and retail users.
   - Utilize social media platforms, crypto forums, and search engine ads to target potential users.
   - Focus on regions and demographics with high adoption potential for DeFi solutions.

### Funding and Governance
- **Allocation**:
  Funding can be supplied as either a percentage of Thorchains revenue (e.g 2%) or via treasury allocation. The specific percentage or amount can be adjusted based on community consensus.

- **Governance**:
  Marketing initiatives and budget allocations will be managed by a dedicated marketing committee represented by active members of the Thorchain developers discord.
  
- **Accountability and transparency**:
  Marketing committee members will provide updates to the Discord and Thorchain community as necessary with a minimum of quarterly reports. These reports will include ROI and gerenal marketing performance to enable the community to assess the effectiveness of campaigns.

### Alternatives Considered
1. **Status Quo**:
   Continuing without dedicated marketing funds risks limited growth and adoption, especially in the face of competition.

2. **Volunteer-Driven Efforts**:
   While cost-effective, volunteer-driven marketing lacks the consistency and scalability needed to make a significant impact.

### Risks
- **Mismanagement of Funds**: To mitigate this risk, all expenditures will require community oversight and approval.
- **Low ROI**: Regular performance evaluations and adjustments to strategy will minimize inefficiencies.

## Next Steps
1. Present this ADR to the Thorchain community and solicit feedback.
2. Establish community members to manage the marketing fund and activities.
3. Begin assessing marketing companies and possible pilot marketing campaigns to measure initial impact to refine strategies.

## Conclusion
Investing in marketing is essential for Thorchain to achieve its long-term vision of becoming the leading decentralized liquidity protocol. By increasing awareness and adoption, a dedicated marketing fund will catalyze growth, enhance the protocol’s reputation, and solidify its position in the DeFi ecosystem.
